# Healthcare Shift Basics Guide

A beginner-friendly manual for healthcare workers learning shift fundamentals: handoffs, report sheets, break planning, and shift recovery. Includes a printable shift-prep checklist and education-only AI aids for drafting notes.

Written plain English, step by step, never condescending — like a friend explaining it to you.

**Published by Asdale Dynamics, LLC** — beginner-friendly AI education, books, guides, and practical tools.

---

## Who this is for

Healthcare workers (nurses, aides, techs, students) who want a clear, practical introduction to the basics of working a shift: how to give and receive handoffs, how to use a report sheet, how to plan breaks, and how to recover after a shift. It's also for anyone who wants education-only help drafting notes with AI — safely, without ever sharing real patient information.

No prior experience needed. If you've never worked a shift before, start here. If you've been working for a while, use it as a refresher.

## How to use it

1. **Read the manual.** Open any file in `docs/` with a text editor or Markdown reader. Start with `docs/shift-prep-checklist.md` for a quick overview, then work through the topic pages.
2. **Open the site.** Open `site/index.html` in any web browser. No install, no build step, no internet needed — it works locally and offline.
3. **Print the checklist.** With `site/index.html` open, use your browser's print command (Ctrl+P / Cmd+P). The print view hides the navigation and shows a clean, printable checklist you can fill out by hand.
4. **Follow your facility.** Every page reminds you: education only, never medical advice, always follow your facility's policy. If this guide and your facility's policy disagree, your facility's policy wins.

## Important notices

**Education only. Never medical advice.** This guide is for learning and planning support. It does not replace facility policy, clinical training, licensure requirements, or professional judgment. Always follow your facility's policies and procedures.

**AI use caution.** AI can make mistakes. Never paste patient identifiers or personal health information into AI tools. AI-generated drafts must be reviewed by a qualified clinician before they go into a real chart.
