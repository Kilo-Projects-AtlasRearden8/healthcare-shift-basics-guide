# Healthcare Shift Basics Guide

A beginner-friendly manual for healthcare workers learning shift fundamentals: handoffs, report sheets, break planning, and shift recovery. Includes a printable shift-prep checklist and education-only AI aids for drafting notes.

**Published by Asdale Dynamics, LLC** — beginner-friendly AI education, books, guides, and practical tools.

## What's inside

- `docs/handoffs.md` — patient handoff basics (education only)
- `docs/report-sheets.md` — how to use and fill out a report sheet
- `docs/break-planning.md` — planning breaks during a shift
- `docs/shift-recovery.md` — recovering after a shift
- `docs/shift-prep-checklist.md` — printable shift-prep checklist
- `docs/ai-notes-aids.md` — education-only AI aids for drafting notes
- `site/` — static HTML site with the printable checklist and AI note aids

## Important notices

**Education only. Never medical advice.** This guide is for learning and planning support. It does not replace facility policy, clinical training, licensure requirements, or professional judgment. Always follow your facility's policies and procedures. If anything here conflicts with your facility's policy, your facility's policy governs.

**AI use caution:** AI can make mistakes. Never paste patient identifiers or personal health information into AI tools. AI-generated drafts must be reviewed by a qualified clinician before use.
