# Shift-Prep Checklist (Printable)

**Education only. Never medical advice.** Use this printable checklist to prepare for your shift. It does not replace facility policy, orientation, or clinical training. Always follow your facility's policies and procedures.

Print this page and check each box as you complete it. Keep it for your personal use only — do not write patient identifiers on it, and dispose of it per facility policy when finished.

---

## Before you leave home

- [ ] Uniform/scrubs clean and ready (per facility dress code)
- [ ] ID badge and facility access credentials
- [ ] Stethoscope, penlight, pens, and any personal tools
- [ ] Watch with a second hand or timing device
- [ ] Comfortable, supportive shoes
- [ ] Water bottle and snacks/meals for the shift
- [ ] Any required certifications/cards (BLS, ACLS, etc.)
- [ ] Reviewed schedule and shift start/end times
- [ ] Arranged transportation and childcare/eldercare as needed
- [ ] Slept adequately before the shift

## On arrival (before your first patient)

- [ ] Checked in and received assignment/patient list
- [ ] Reviewed assignment for acuity and special precautions (isolation, fall risk, etc.)
- [ ] Located emergency equipment (crash cart, fire extinguishers, exits)
- [ ] Identified charge nurse, preceptor, and team members for the shift
- [ ] Reviewed any unit-specific updates or policy changes
- [ ] Set up your report sheet (blank, no patient info yet)
- [ ] Checked your break plan and coverage arrangements
- [ ] Confirmed your login credentials for the EHR/workstations

## During handoff/report

- [ ] Received handoff in your facility's required format (SBAR, I-PASS, etc.)
- [ ] Took notes on your report sheet
- [ ] Asked clarifying questions
- [ ] Confirmed code status, allergies, and isolation for each patient
- [ ] Noted pending tests, procedures, and medications due
- [ ] Identified time-critical tasks for the shift
- [ ] Documented the handoff per facility policy (if required)

## Mid-shift

- [ ] Took scheduled breaks with coverage arranged
- [ ] Kept report sheet updated as events occurred
- [ ] Rechecked time-critical tasks and med due times
- [ ] Escalated any patient changes to the appropriate provider/charge nurse
- [ ] Documented care per facility policy and timing requirements

## Before handoff to the next shift

- [ ] Completed outstanding tasks or delegated them clearly
- [ ] Updated your report sheet with current status
- [ ] Gave handoff in your facility's required format
- [ ] Invited questions and confirmed read-backs where required
- [ ] Documented the handoff per facility policy (if required)
- [ ] Returned equipment and restocked supplies you used

## End of shift

- [ ] Signed out of all workstations/EHR
- [ ] Collected personal belongings
- [ ] Secured or destroyed any paper notes per facility policy (shred or secure bin)
- [ ] Left the work area tidy for the next shift
- [ ] Clocked out per facility timekeeping policy

## After the shift

- [ ] Debriefed anything difficult with a trusted person (no patient details in public)
- [ ] Hydrated and ate
- [ ] Wrote down anything still on your mind ("brain dump") to close out the day
- [ ] Planned rest and recovery before your next shift
